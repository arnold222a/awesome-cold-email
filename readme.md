# Awesome Cold Email [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of awesome cold email resources: tools, deliverability, copywriting, data and research.

Cold email is permission-less B2B outreach done right: relevant, personalized, compliant and measurable. This list collects the tools, guides, benchmarks, datasets and legal references that practitioners actually use.

## Tools & Platforms

### Sending Platforms

- [Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai/) - Cold email platform with unlimited sender accounts, inbox rotation and a unified master inbox.
- [Instantly](https://instantly.ai/) - Sending platform focused on scale, with built-in warmup pool and campaign analytics.
- [Lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com/) - Outreach platform with image and landing page personalization plus multichannel sequences.
- [Woodpecker](https://woodpecker.co/) - Cold email tool for agencies and SMBs with condition-based sequences and deliverability checks.
- [Mailshake](https://mailshake.com/) - Sales engagement platform for email, phone and social touchpoints.
- [QuickMail](https://quickmail.com/) - Outreach automation with inbox rotation and native deliverability reports.
- [Saleshandy](https://www.saleshandy.com/) - Cold email software with sequence scoring, unified inbox and agency features.
- [GMass](https://www.gmass.co/) - Mail merge and cold email campaigns that run directly inside Gmail.
- [Klenty](https://www.klenty.com/) - Sales engagement platform with cadences across email, calls and LinkedIn.
- [Outreach](https://www.outreach.io/) - Enterprise sales execution platform with sequences, analytics and forecasting.

### Prospecting & Verification

- [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io/) - B2B contact database with search filters, enrichment and built-in sequences.
- [Hunter](https://hunter.io/) - Email finder and verifier with domain search and API.
- [Snov.io](https://snov.io/) - Prospect search, email finder, verifier and drip campaigns.
- [Clay](https://www.clay.com/) - Data enrichment workspace that combines dozens of providers with AI research agents.
- [ZeroBounce](https://www.zerobounce.net/) - Email validation service with activity data and deliverability toolkit.
- [MillionVerifier](https://www.millionverifier.com/) - Bulk email verification service with pay-as-you-go pricing.
- [Datacircle](https://datacircle.dev/best-email-finder-api) - Benchmarks of email finder and verification APIs, ranked by accuracy and what 1,000 calls cost.

## Deliverability

- [Google Postmaster Tools](https://postmaster.google.com/) - Google's official dashboard for domain reputation, spam rate and delivery errors on Gmail.
- [Microsoft SNDS](https://sendersupport.olc.protection.outlook.com/snds/) - Smart Network Data Services, Microsoft's view of your sending IP reputation on Outlook and Hotmail.
- [Mailreach](https://www.mailreach.co/) - Email warmup and spam testing service with a reputation score per mailbox.
- [GlockApps](https://glockapps.com/) - Inbox placement tests across providers with DMARC analytics.
- [MXToolbox](https://mxtoolbox.com/) - Free lookups for MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and blacklist monitoring.
- [Mail-tester](https://www.mail-tester.com/) - Free one-shot spam score test for any outgoing email.
- [Folderly](https://folderly.com/) - Deliverability platform that monitors and fixes inbox placement issues.
- [Warmup Inbox](https://www.warmupinbox.com/) - Automated inbox warming network for new sending domains.
- [Learn DMARC](https://www.learndmarc.com/) - Interactive visualizer that shows how SPF, DKIM and DMARC validate a real email.
- [dmarcian](https://dmarcian.com/) - DMARC management platform and free record checking tools.
- [DMARC Digests by Postmark](https://dmarc.postmarkapp.com/) - Weekly human-readable DMARC reports.
- [Spamhaus](https://www.spamhaus.org/) - The reference blocklist operator; check if your IPs or domains are listed.

- [Close cold email guide](https://www.close.com/blog/cold-email) - Long-form guide on writing cold emails that get responses, with templates and teardowns.
- [Lemlist cold email templates](https://www.lemlist.com/cold-email-templates) - Library of real campaign templates with reported metrics.
- [Josh Braun](https://joshbraun.com/) - Sales trainer known for low-pressure, curiosity-based outbound messaging.
- [30 Minutes to President's Club](https://www.30mpc.com/) - Podcast and resources with tactical outbound and cold outreach playbooks.

## Data & Benchmarks

- [Woodpecker cold email statistics](https://woodpecker.co/blog/cold-email-statistics/) - Benchmarks from millions of cold emails sent through the platform: open, reply and bounce rates.
- [Instantly cold email statistics](https://instantly.ai/blog/cold-email-statistics) - Aggregated campaign statistics and benchmark numbers from Instantly users.
- [Belkins cold email response rates](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates) - Agency benchmarks on response rates by industry and campaign type.
- [Backlinko email outreach study](https://backlinko.com/email-outreach-study) - Analysis of 12 million outreach emails: what affects reply rates, subject lines and follow-ups.

## Datasets & Research

- [Enron Email Dataset](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~enron/) - The classic public corpus of ~500k real corporate emails, widely used in email research and NLP.
- [Avocado Research Email Collection](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2015T03) - LDC corpus of corporate emails and attachments used in academic email studies.
- [Osservatorio Cold Email Italia](https://github.com/arnold222a/osservatorio-cold-email-italia) - Open dataset (CC BY 4.0, with DOI) of 723 real Italian B2B cold email campaigns with per-campaign metrics; interactive version at [clientium.it/osservatorio](https://clientium.it/osservatorio/).

## Legal & Compliance

- [GDPR full text (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) - Official text of Regulation (EU) 2016/679; legitimate interest is the usual basis for B2B outreach in the EU.
- [GDPR.eu](https://gdpr.eu/) - Plain-language explainers and checklists on GDPR obligations.
- [CAN-SPAM compliance guide (FTC)](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) - Official US requirements for commercial email: identification, opt-out and truthful headers.
- [PECR direct marketing guidance (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/) - UK rules on electronic direct marketing from the Information Commissioner's Office.
- [CASL (CRTC)](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/internet/anti.htm) - Canada's anti-spam legislation: consent requirements for commercial electronic messages.
