# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for wanting to contribute to this list! Please make sure your suggestion follows these guidelines before opening a pull request.

## Adding a resource

- Search previous suggestions before making a new one, so yours is not a duplicate.
- One resource per pull request, with a useful title (for example `Add GlockApps to Deliverability`).
- Add the link in the format `- [Name](url) - Description.` at the bottom of the relevant section.
- The description should be one sentence, neutral and factual. No marketing superlatives.
- Descriptions start with a capital letter and end with a period.
- The resource must be alive, publicly accessible and genuinely useful for cold email practitioners.
- Vendor links are fine if the resource is notable and widely used, but no affiliate links.
- No resources that promote spam, scraped personal data of consumers, or practices that violate GDPR, CAN-SPAM, PECR or CASL.

## Quality standards

Ask yourself: would a practitioner who just discovered cold email thank you for this link? If the answer is not a clear yes, it probably does not belong here.

## Updating or removing a resource

Pull requests that fix dead links, outdated descriptions or misplaced entries are always welcome.

## Style

- Check your spelling and grammar.
- Keep the alphabetical or logical order of the section you are editing.
- Remove trailing whitespace.
